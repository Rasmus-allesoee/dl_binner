## What improved and what failed

With E022 E009 learning fixed, R95 rises **7.7497% -> 10.4163%**: +14,790,857 passing
reference bp; targets at 50% rise 14 -> 19 and at 90% 8 -> 9. E023 exceeds retained
MetaBAT2's **8.5493%** by 10,355,901 bp / 1.8671 percentage points on this declared
E009 metric. This closes that scoped gap; it is not a general SOTA/native-eligibility
or unseen-community claim. E023 has 18 gaining / 8 losing targets versus E022.
Do not hide individual losses behind the aggregate, or reduce full denominators.

Oral E019-vector R95 instead falls **6.5077% -> 4.0932%**, -50,296,592 bp; targets
at 50% fall 43 -> 30 and at 90% 33 -> 10, with 25 gains / 57 losses. Density-only
oral is 3.5833%; cached marker weighting adds 10,621,143 bp, six target gains and
no target losses within this hierarchy. E009's two partitions are byte-identical.
That supports retaining the marker mechanism as useful, not crediting markers
for E009's gain or promoting the whole rule despite oral harm. Original joint
**MODIFY** is unchanged. No per-community truth-selected product router is adopted.

All 156 / 554,661,890 bp and 799 / 2,083,072,211 bp reference populations remain.
E009 N quarantine remains 7,653,205 bp / 3.3034%. Oral is a provided-origin oracle;
known-shared masking leaves these recovery endpoints unchanged but is not an
exhaustive uniqueness test. Native MetaBAT2's 2,500 bp seed / 1,000 bp later
eligibility differs from our 500 bp eligibility. Its inputs/truth/DNA were checked
in the separately retained secondary review; neither baseline was rescored.

Secondary target-dominant threshold-based HQ/MQ/LQ counts: **E023 E009 9/10/10**
(all bins 10/16/16), retained **MetaBAT2 5/11/6** (all 7/11/9), and **oral 10/21/24**.
All-bin, target-only and >=200 kb tables retain the other/impure/unresolved bins.
These are sequence-union completeness and conservative impurity tiers, not formal
MIMAG HQ. Confirmed foreign-origin and unresolved DNA are separate; no RNA screen.
Integer E023 bin IDs in the tables are zero-based partition IDs (FASTA names add
one); MetaBAT2 labels retain their native names.

## Diagnose information, geometry and selection separately

The independent [cached-neighbor audit](../../experiments/E023-adaptive-density-bins/comparison-review.json)
compares the first five **retained composite** neighbors with the preserved native
learned five-neighbor summaries on exactly the same eligible attributable queries.
No neighbor search or partition was rerun. E009 has 76,010 such queries; oral
508,763. Unknown queries and all excluded/unassigned DNA remain separately counted.

| Matched query population | Preserved learned bp-weighted same-source fraction | New composite fraction |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| E009 all attributable eligible | 79.7119% | 88.2803% |
| E009 bacterial attributable eligible | 81.8251% | 85.8166% |
| Oral provided-origin eligible | 20.7688% | 20.3297% |

The much larger unweighted overall E009 gain is partly eukaryotic; the bacterial
row prevents calling that entire gain a primary-target improvement. Retrieval
recall (99.6875% / 98.3333% on 32 queries) checks the approximation in its declared
index geometry, not biological homogeneity, exact composite-neighbor optimality
or genome recovery. Oral remains weak even above 10 kb (19.4067% bp-weighted
same-origin fraction in the new neighbors). Another search-accuracy threshold
will not manufacture independent information.

The oral flat partition also merges many origins: B3 precision falls from E019's
0.2565 to 0.1092. Density persistence and the current
soft marker factor are not calibrated biological confidence. Source review finds
no demonstrated critical ID/cache/percent-scale/missing-depth/geometry adapter
bug: native reader/reload and exact source contracts pass; empty oral depth is
omitted rather than treated as measured zero. This does not prove the heuristic
optimal or exonerate all untested algorithmic assumptions.

E009 residual **confirmed query support**, not new duplicate-safe recovery, is
19.610 Mb unassigned, 17.428 Mb in dominant but below-95%-purity bins, 1.915 Mb as
minor origins in mixed bins, 6.490 Mb excluded, and only 0.135 Mb in pure dominant
bins below 200 kb. A further small-bin merge tweak is therefore not the principal
remaining opportunity. Unresolved sequence dominates much of the conservative
impurity; it is not all demonstrated foreign DNA. No bins or thresholds change
from these retrospective categories.

Depth is not causally identified by the between-community contrast. E009 and
oral differ in community, assembly/length population, truth and upstream learning
(including the E009 prior and depth-gated links). E022 does not activate those
links on depth-free oral. This is exactly why a within-oral information comparison
is more informative now than asserting that absent depth caused the regression.

## Targeted new primary/source audit

The [MetaCAT primary abstract/public Extended Data Fig. 1](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41564-026-02472-7)
and independent author source at `5de573fbce0a6549010b07002e278620a7da22b9` were
checked. Main subscription methods were not newly read. [Exact source bindings](../../experiments/E023-adaptive-density-bins/external-review.json)
cover composition, coverage, affinities, seed/sequence models, scoring and dispatch.
Only an unrelated untracked `.DS_Store` exists; inspected files match that root's
commit, not the enclosing project's Git history.

Its code uses a fitted length-dependent composition log probability, explicit
coverage means/variances, biological seed/marker scoring and conditional graph or
mixture processing. This supports independent abundance and evidence calibration
as serious alternatives to network size. It is not permission to copy a variance
model onto mean-only features, impose naive repeated-marker cannot-links, import
its grid, run MetaCAT or treat its paper's results as reproduced here. E011/E012
paralog/replicon/partial-call failures still constrain marker use.

[Official HDBSCAN documentation](https://hdbscan.readthedocs.io/en/latest/how_hdbscan_works.html)
clarifies the density/MST/condensation/stability rationale. E023 is explicitly a
sparse bp-weighted, whole-branch heuristic; stability does not establish a genome
boundary. No unexpected departure from the prospectively declared E023 rule was
found. Calibrated marker-anchored mixtures remain a serious later alternative if
new information is adequate but the same density rule still fails.

## Strongest next block: real oral abundance, then one fixed-learning control

**Prepare genuine measured ten-sample depth on the existing oral GSA and one
prospective cached information comparison.** Keep the current encoder, native
vectors, seed and cached physical-marker policy fixed. Preserve sequence-only
outputs as the comparator; this first comparison tests downstream information,
not depth training or a new representation. It is a decision-changing data/input
block, not another small loss or resolution sweep.

Use all ten publisher samples **6,7,8,13,14,15,16,17,18,19**. Previous actual HEADs
sum to **51,731,811,351 bytes / 48.18 GiB** compressed FASTQ; refresh publisher
metadata/checksums before any acquisition. [The existing assessment](../../experiments/E014-multiview-reconstruction/depth-assessment.md)
checked that supplied BAMs align to source genomes, not these GSA contigs. They
cannot supply model depth through truth-coordinate projection. Map real reads to
the exact opaque-ID GSA, declaring multihit/filter/summary semantics without using
origin labels. Preserve all DNA and the provided-origin/competitive-ambiguity
limits. Contig means are the first feature; any saved variance must be genuinely
measured and separately described, never invented window variance.

Preparation must resolve how depth enters candidate retrieval as well as final
reranking. A restrictive sequence-only pool can conceal abundance's value. If
an observed-modality candidate union is required, freeze and declare that coherent
bundle and verify the exact no-depth path; do not claim a pure reweighting control
or silently change the comparator. Do not retrain, redetect markers or extract
oral GLM crops. A fresh depth-rebind receipt must prove exact DNA/ID/TNF/view/native
vector equality, bind both caches, and leave original artifacts untouched.

The old all-ten estimate is 8–24 hours / 12–26 GB RSS: roughly **$4–10 mapping-only**,
not a fresh native forecast. A **new ~$12–15 complete pilot/mapping/cached-control
request** could be reasonable after preparation; no budget is approved here.
Current native evidence has only ~51.53 GiB free, ~26.53 GiB above reserve. Earlier
45–130 GiB serial estimates retained BAMs and do not fit their upper end. A tested
stream-one-sample plan, validated compact summaries and an explicit intermediate
BAM retention/cleanup policy may reduce the peak; measure index/sort/BAM coexistence
and refuse if it cannot fit. No deletion or resource increase occurs in inspection.
Public reads are not to be staged on this Mac. Fresh credit/product/price/disk/
clock admission and a separately approved native pilot remain required.

The fixed scientific comparison should materially improve the oral information
and recovery without losing complete accounting or silently weakening truth.
Freeze practical keep/modify/abandon criteria before its changed outcomes,
including comparison to both the preserved no-depth E023 and E019 context. If
depth is uninformative or the density policy still fails, use that evidence to
choose calibrated biological bin construction versus representation changes;
do not launch automatic retries, grids or another model in the same data budget.

Why this before the main alternatives:

- A larger Transformer or another full oral GLM crop bank adds substantial runtime;
  current evidence does not identify capacity as the decisive missing ingredient.
  E021's extra prior gave a narrow mixed gain and its full oral bank never ran.
- Another density/marker-penalty sweep risks fitting these same two communities.
  Better source homogeneity and independent information should be assessed first.
- Marker-anchored probabilistic bin construction is promising, but it cannot
  guarantee source discrimination from weak redundant composition alone and must
  respect observed same-genome marker-copy exceptions.
- A zero-depth E009 control is cheaper but still has depth-trained/prior-enriched
  vectors and a different community; it cannot answer the within-oral question.
- Broad new datasets or an external-tool campaign add less immediate design
  evidence than this missing measurement. Do not postpone independent confirmation
  indefinitely: both current communities are development data.
