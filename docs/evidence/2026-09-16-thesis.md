## Workflow vocabulary

These are the thesis's definitions; do not transfer a paper's word "multisample" without examining its actual pipeline.

| Mode | Coverage information | Clustering population |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SSM | Own-sample reads mapped to its assembly | One sample |
| MAM | All relevant samples' reads mapped against each sample assembly separately | One sample at a time |
| CS | Reads mapped to concatenated per-sample assemblies | Pooled contigs, followed by splitting bins by sample |
| SC | Concatenated-reference coverage; representation may be learned globally | Split by sample before clustering |

Co-assembly is a different choice: reads are assembled jointly. Concatenating independent assemblies is not co-assembly. For each new experiment, record assembly source, mapping reference, sample columns, training population, and clustering population separately.

## Limits that change our research choices

1. **The old simulation is diagnostic, not a general benchmark.** Sections 2.2 and 4.8 describe 64 reference genomes, seven strain pairs, five samples, a fixed-abundance background, and digitally shredded 2-20 kb fragments. Near-identical copies in pooled inputs produced severe CS failures. Reuse this to study that mechanism, not as the sole basis for choosing a new binner.
2. **ANI and genome identity are confounded.** There is one selected pair per target ANI region. The observed failure at 99.5/99.9% ANI does not establish a universal biological or algorithmic limit. A new strain experiment would need independent pairs, mapping checks, and explicit treatment of shared sequence.
3. **The minimum-length filter limits what we know.** The real benchmark excluded contigs below 2 kb. It cannot establish how modern short-contig methods compare throughout their intended range. Shared-input comparisons and native recommended settings answer different questions; both may eventually be needed.
4. **The simulation does not establish transferability.** Reported stability of quality-estimator rankings within that simulation is not validation in other ecosystems. Neither "FDR is a lower bound in real data" nor "relative ranking transfers" should be inherited as established facts.
5. **Matched-genome comparisons still need denominators.** Comparing only genomes recovered by both tools can hide failures and unique recovery. Include a fixed eligible truth set, missing recoveries, and an explicit recoverability analysis in new simulations.
6. **Patches are part of the method actually evaluated.** The thesis documents COMEBin and GenomeFace compatibility changes, and a CompleteBin restoration that resolves duplicate ownership using bin size. This restoration changes assignments; it is not necessarily equivalent to an upstream repair. Check current releases and preserve raw/repaired outputs if a repair is still required.

## Specific interpretations to verify before reuse

- **Coverage scale versus coverage information.** Lower absolute depth does not by itself prove a worse learned representation: some transformations remove scale. Test whether relative profiles, zero patterns, or uncertainty change after each tool's normalization, while holding the clustering population fixed. This is our inference motivating [H001](../research/questions.md#h001), not a new result.
- **Marker usage in COMEBin.** Supplement S3.2 describes COMEBin as marker-agnostic during partitioning, whereas its method and local clustering entry point use a marker seed file. Avoid inheriting the blanket categorization; inspect the exact stages/version. See the [code note](2026-09-16-landscape.md#limited-code-inspection).
- **Failure archetypes are conditional.** Merger/fragmenter descriptions vary between thesis sections and settings. Record observed merge, split, unassigned, and mixed-bin errors per regime rather than treating an archetype as a permanent tool property.
- **Contamination definitions differ.** AMBER origin-based impurity and CheckM2 contamination are different estimands and can use different denominators. A new audit must document formulas before directly comparing values or building a composite score.

These qualifications preserve the thesis's usefulness while preventing a previous interpretation from becoming an untested design requirement.
