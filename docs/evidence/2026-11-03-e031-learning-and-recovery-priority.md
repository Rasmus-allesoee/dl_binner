## Source-grounded next decision

Choose one **common biological candidate-selection and joint-recruitment upgrade**,
holding E031 learning fixed. Reuse the two retained candidate hierarchies, their
whole/child alternatives and physical marker evidence. Use reliable marker-quality
proxies together with composition, measured parent-depth and explicit availability/
length context. Resolve overlaps and evaluate recruited groups at bin level; keep
an unchanged whole-bin alternative. Do not merely union emitted bins or route each
dataset to its known winner. [The next-block proposal](../../experiments/E031-bootstrap-preserving-learning/next-block.md)
owns its prospective implementation, resource case and execution request.

A targeted [DAS Tool paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6786971/) and
[pinned source audit](https://github.com/cmks/DAS_Tool/blob/846d0dc09ddf46eef151dcf1aa4cdb3509b0a857/src/DAS_Tool.R)
supports candidate-level integration as an established design pattern. At
`cherry_pick` / `score_bins` / `calc_bin_score`, it ranks bins using single-copy
gene proxies, removes claimed contigs from competitors and rescores affected bins.
Its marker-only score and threshold are not adopted: our call-specificity/lineage
limits and marker-negative DNA require additional context and explicit abstention.
