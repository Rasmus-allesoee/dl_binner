## Problem and evidence

The usable first CPU prototype is merged and tested, but E003/E004 are controlled
development fragments and E005 is a four-genome development GSA derivative.
Independent read-derived whole-reference/binning quality and assembly-scale costs
remain unknown. The owner wants a high-performing, potentially SOTA binner, and
describes the current implementation as a poor prototype. That is a development
assessment, not a newly measured universal ranking. Software acceptance is a
completed milestone; competitive binning performance remains the goal.

The concern about paying to establish an expected mature-tool advantage is valid.
Thesis results, published benchmarks and reviewed source already establish credible
alternatives. An expensive ranking alone would supply little causal architecture
evidence. E009 must add **assembly-specific, reusable data and diagnostic evidence
that can change an improvement decision**, without becoming final evaluation or
a compulsory tool campaign before development. Reusable reads/assembly/BAMs/depth
are valuable even when some or all external fits are deferred.

Reuse the completed review/source audit and accepted E001–E008 records.
[The October focused study](../evidence/2026-10-08-competitive-binner-study.md)
separates inputs/pretraining, learning objective, network architecture and
grouping/refinement for all seven owner-ranked tools. It identifies actual sequence
views/negative handling, biological grouping and sequence context as serious
alternatives, with source and evidence limits explicit. A tool ranking is not a
causal component experiment. The owner expects a mature-tool advantage over the
prototype; quantifying it is useful only when retained diagnostics can change an
improvement decision. Software acceptance and one comparison do not establish
competitive performance or a universal architecture/feature-family conclusion.

## Choice and alternatives

MEGAHIT assembly from **CAMI III Toy Longitudinal Human Gut short-read sample 0**,

These are the first listed samples of each of the ten corrected subjects;

representation/cost tradeoff, not evidence that ten is biologically optimal.

later but shares old source populations/plumbing. Toy III offers a more current
gut community without active-challenge restrictions. At the October-9 review, the active III challenge had

CAMISIM remains a targeted mechanism instrument after a real observed deficit,

They cannot decide whether depth, sequence or taxonomy belongs in future versions.
Keep full short/noise/excluded DNA and per-length/per-origin diagnostics.

## Cost and decision limits

Use local Mac for cheap adapter checks and returned-output analysis; direct
verified GCP for separately authorized bulk work. The old 700 UCloud core-hour
plan/balance, Home/shared paths and 16/48 allocation are
[archived planning](../history/E009_UCLOUD_OPERATIONS.md#superseded-two-depth-resource-protocol),
not monetary GCP credits. Propose an 8-vCPU/32-GiB serial data stage, 300-GiB
working disk and 24-hour cumulative driver clock/25-hour provider STOP, with a
$20 gross run plus seven-day retention envelope. Current CPU quota fits; request balanced/SSD quota >=300 GiB before growth;
full fits need a separate measured monetary/storage/RAM admission. Ordinary
trial-credit eligibility, localized costs and fresh displayed balance/scope must
be checked before spending; no growth/retry follows a failure automatically.

CompleteBin's per-base coverage/training duplication and retained 105/36
clustering grids need explicit peak accounting. One assembly avoids twenty run
directories but still uses ten BAMs; max-50 reduces checkpoints/time, not coverage
or candidate storage. [The audit](../evidence/2026-10-14-completebin-storage.md)
retains source evidence and a later 400–500-GiB/64–128-GiB possibility, not a new
provisioning order or hidden change to its scientific schedule.

Use AMBER on its validated contig truth view and alignment-supported conservative
whole-reference recovery with all-bin purity denominators and duplicate-safe
coordinate unions. Keep assembly-conditional recovery separate. Real-assembly
truth adapters now pass real small-DNA local tests, with Linux/scale unvalidated; E005's GSA evaluator
cannot be applied unchanged. Pin minimap2's all-chain PAF/CIGAR output for the
narrow adapter instead of depending on MetaQUAST's filtered internal alignments;
MetaQUAST remains a possible supplemental misassembly report. Whole *provided reference* recovery is not formal
MIMAG quality or completeness of an unknown finished genome when a source is a MAG.

Marker-aware grouping/refinement is a **high-priority** next product candidate:
it directly addresses the accepted prototype's fragmented grouping, preserves the
encoder and can reuse embeddings. Plan one fixed marker-conflict splitting/merging
ablation against identical frozen grouping, retaining marker-negative and short
DNA and evaluating with independent sequence truth. Marker-aware training remains
a serious alternative, not an automatic simultaneous addition.

If learning damages raw signal, prioritize one view/negative-construction change;
if embeddings separate but bins fail, prioritize one grouping/refinement ablation;
if short-sequence information remains limiting, the planned matched TNF/GLM-head/
hybrid comparison is a strong next candidate. Its complementary-information
rationale is retained, not assumed proven. Do not adopt several mechanisms from
one native-tool victory or tune to E009 and claim held-out validation.
