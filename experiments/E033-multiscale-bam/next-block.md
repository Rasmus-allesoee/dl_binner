# Proposed next block: BAM-supported genome-graph representation

November 5, 2026. Recommendation after [completed E033 review](completed-review.md),
not implementation or execution authority. [Source/alternatives review](../../docs/evidence/2026-11-05-e033-genome-graph-priority.md)
owns the reasoning. Allocate a new experiment/source freeze when this work starts;
do not modify the accepted E033 candidate to turn ABANDON into a win.

## Scientific scope

Build one common binner upgrade around physical fragment evidence. Preserve the
stronger E031 learning and provisional E032 downstream policy as the primary
bootstrap/comparator. Keep E023 E009 GLM 10.4163%, the stronger E031 constructor
contexts, both E032 modes and native MetaBAT2 results as unrescored anchors.
The weaker E033 outputs remain evidence, not the new quality hurdle.

Use retained E031 source-bound unit vectors as frozen node inputs, available raw
composition, explicitly new shared E033 measured parent/window context, and GLM
only where its admitted policy permits it. Historical depth priors in the frozen
vectors remain visible. Do not refit the E031/E033 encoders or regenerate BAM
moments, views, markers or the E009 frozen GLM bank. New graph representations
are a declared treatment; retain their inputs, embeddings, full graph/edge reasons
and pre/post partitions. A frozen-vector input is not a claim of identical final
geometry after graph learning.

Implement a scalable template-link reader/cache and a compact confidence-gated
graph encoder. Resolve precise architecture, loss, normalization and defaults from
targeted primary sources during implementation, and freeze them before changed
community outcomes. Do not automatically hard-union a connected component, infer
truth K, use native labels, or add taxonomy/ensembles/marker cannot-links. Similarity
edges and physical edges must remain distinguishable; uncertainty or missing links
must have a valid unlinked path. Do not use an unreliable similarity edge as a
physical positive merely to make a graph dense. At most one prospective links-off
control is justified if it changes the design decision, preserving module/RNG
contracts and avoiding another factorial campaign.

The paired-template contract must independently verify both actual mates,
per-sample opaque QNAME identity, R1/R2 roles, flags, MAPQ/NM/CIGAR consistency,
reference and coordinate bounds, orientation/end/insert evidence, duplicate and
multi-primary ambiguity, and template-count normalization. A mate pointer alone
is insufficient; cross-contig pairs need not carry the proper-pair flag. Reads
from different samples with equal QNAME are not mates. Simulator/read headers can
expose truth: never parse their genome/accession/coordinate fields as features.
Use hashes/opaque join keys and keep biological labels outside all model inputs.
Count templates, retain rejection/repeat/hub ledgers, and normalize so coverage
or repeats cannot dominate by raw link count. Test name collation/external joins
without silently assuming coordinate-sorted mates are adjacent.

Use actual tiny BAM/SAM fixtures, independent expected template counts, malformed
NM/flags/axes/duplicate/cross-sample/unmapped/refusal cases, split/missing mates,
repeat hubs, disconnected and all-unlinked graphs, sparse/readback/permutation
contracts, actual tiny graph training/gradient/reload/geometry and complete DNA
exports. Test the actual installed wheel and archive → compact staging → strict
reader path. Do not mock the failing production path and call it native validation.

## Admission before any future community fit

The first native pilot is scientific admission of the information source, not a
performance sweep. Use one source-blind representative retained BAM per community,
then serially complete the fixed cache only if the frozen reader/support gate and
full schedule pass. Quantify retained/rejected templates, unique supported pairs,
linked contig/bp/length/availability coverage, components, repeats, source-specific
support and actual RAM/space/throughput. A GSA can have no useful read-pair bridges;
no assembly graph is assumed. Freeze one label-free utility/reliability rule before
pilot results. If it fails, preserve the failure and reconsider before fitting;
do not lower the criterion, invent links or replace it with a truth-selected cutoff.
Truth may diagnose the fixed artifact afterward, never select edges or thresholds.

Freeze one prospective MAG-recovery decision rule before candidate outcomes.
Require no material common-mode regression against E032 and meaningful recovery
beyond strong E009 references, plus fragmented/multi-contig Oral improvement;
report every trade-off. Merely beating rejected E033 is insufficient. Keep practical
thresholds distinct from biological standards. Preserve full 156/799 denominators,
conservative E009 v2 / 3.3034% N quarantine, separately named Oral origin/known-
shared sensitivity and all short/marker-negative/unlinked/unknown/non-target/
excluded/unassigned/singleton/multi-contig populations. Use only new scores; old
summaries remain unchanged. A bundle gain is not proof isolating the graph source
without the prospective matched control.
