## Improvement decision

Retain E031 as the fixed development learner in all three modes. Implement one
shared bin constructor using supported candidates from its cached E023/E030
hierarchies, rather than choosing a historical pipeline by dataset. This is an
internal common candidate policy, not an external ensemble campaign. The same
vectors give opposite construction trade-offs on E009 and Oral; final updates
also destroy otherwise recoverable targets. A coherent candidate-selection and
group-level update rule addresses those observed failures without another fit.

Resolve precise source-blind algorithms/defaults during implementation. Use
cached physical-call reliability, family/locus context, composition and real
parent-contig depth. Preserve whole parents, supported children and pre-update
alternatives. Treat marker scores as imperfect quality proxies, with lineage and
panel limits; provide a path for coherent marker-negative candidates. Distinguish
positive, observed-zero and unavailable evidence, and contig-length reliability.
Evaluate a proposed group of recruits jointly against the resulting bin profile.
Any variance describes observed contig profiles, not invented per-base/window
coverage. Preserve all DNA, including short/unknown/non-target and unassigned.

Use compact hierarchy/membership and additive profile representations. Do not
materialize every ancestor's full contig set or perform an exhaustive candidate
subset/grid search. Source-derived heuristics must be frozen before new scores.
Use actual tiny overlap/whole-child/recruitment/export tests and a bounded
synthetic scale contract. Retain candidate, selected and pre/post partitions.

The cheap within-run pre/post comparison is sufficient initially; no mandatory
factorial or extra learning treatment is needed. Compare the single common result
beside both E031 constructors and the immutable E023/E026/MetaBAT2 summaries.
Freeze keep/modify/abandon criteria prospectively; never use truth to choose bins,
K, thresholds, families, cores or routing. The truth-labelled 14.0239% Oral
five-partition envelope is a diagnostic, not an attainable performance promise.
It supports complementary candidates and shows that pooling final bins alone
cannot erase the entire MetaBAT2 gap.

## Alternatives and revisit conditions

- More learning/larger architecture: remains open. E031 improves previous students
  but teacher changes under the strong constructor are small, while constructor
  effects remain large. Prefer the directly observed construction problem now;
  revisit representation if the new candidate pool lacks recoverable genomes or
  cannot improve the shared rule.
- Only protect recruitment: useful but too narrow. Oral's pre-selection and the
  E009 constructor gap also matter, so selection and group-level update acceptance
  belong in one block.
- Full Oral GLM: important for the intended main-mode information contract. The
  current 128-view L4 attempt is capacity-blocked, not failed numerically. Do not
  infer throughput or wait indefinitely for that exact placement. The separately
  priced existing T4 route below changes hardware availability, not DNA exposure.
- More datasets/external tools: preserve unscored skin and its admissions. They
  are not a substitute for understanding the present matched failures. Native
  external expansion and new DNA remain excluded from this block.
