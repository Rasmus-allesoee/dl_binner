## Supported grouping

Build a native graph from each new unit embedding for proposal provenance/support.
For downstream bin construction, one sparse all-eligible joint index uses graded
native unit distance, Hellinger TNF and bounded log-parent-depth distance. Separate
label-free fixed hash cohorts set positive squared-distance medians. Use between-
parent log-depth standard deviations, then tanh coordinates to bound outliers.
Distance is sqrt[(native/median + r*composition/median + (1-r) + depth)/3], where
r=sqrt[L_i/(L_i+2500) * L_j/(L_j+2500)]. Positive/positive and positive/observed-zero
pairs use their measured depth contrast; co-zero or unavailable depth contributes
neutral 1. Channels are correlated, not independent common-genome probabilities.
There is no per-channel rank veto, reciprocal-core-only exclusion or truth K.

NNDescent seed 11, 61 discovery neighbors, 60 candidates, 15 iterations, initialized
from this arm's native graph. Consume its training-neighbor graph once, avoiding the
known slow external-query path. Exact float64 top-30 rerank; frozen sampled exhaustive
recall >=0.90 overall and in populated positive/zero/short/long strata. Persist numeric
failed evidence before refusal; never silently widen or lower a gate.

The retained E023 sparse mutual-reachability forest and bp-persistence condensation
provide whole/child alternatives; soft physical-marker breadth/excess weighting
(5x excess penalty) selects one disjoint antichain. Preserve density-only parent
and marker-selected post partitions. A leaf is not assigned just for being long:
a >=200 kb leaf needs two reliable (>=0.8) physical families and positive persistence
bounded by its own kNN radius. Multi-contig marker-negative candidates keep their
density path. Minimum supported mass stays 10 kb. No universal two-contig ban,
automatic singleton assignment, new marker search or truth-selected bin routing.
All short, excluded, unknown/non-target, marker-negative and unassigned DNA survives.
